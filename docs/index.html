How the list was made

Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Breezy, Recruitee and Teamtailor each publish a job board API that needs no key and no account. Give it a company's board token and it answers with that company's open postings. The hard part is not reading a board — it is knowing which tokens exist.

Those came from the Common Crawl web indexes, CC-MAIN-2024-51 through CC-MAIN-2025-51: every URL on the public web pointing at one of the six board hosts, reduced to the distinct token. On Greenhouse, Ashby and Lever the token sits in the path; on Breezy, Recruitee and Teamtailor it is the subdomain. That harvest is a list of candidates, not of companies.

Every candidate was then called directly, once. 18,164 answered with at least one open posting and are listed here. 3,958 answered correctly with an empty board — real companies, nothing open that day — and are not listed. The rest did not answer and were dropped.

So the count beside each company is not an estimate. It is what that endpoint returned on 2026-09-03. Call it yourself and you should get a similar number, drifting as they hire.

One honest limit: a board is only findable here if something on the public web linked to it at some point between late 2024 and late 2025. Companies that never linked their board anywhere crawlable are missing, and there is no way to count what you cannot see.

  • data-quality.csv500 rows — which boards carry postings that are not paid jobs. 1,411 commission-only recruitment ads sit on 2 boards; one of them is 79% ads
  • board-roles.csv500 rows — the role family each board’s own postings imply, with the confidence it was inferred at. Blank where the evidence is too thin to say; 101 boards clear the bar
  • board-industry.csv142 rows — boards whose industry is readable from the board token alone. 81 keywords were tested against 838 boards’ real postings and 8 survived; each row says whether the check agreed
  • duplication-corpus.csv7 rows — how much of the corpus is one board posting the same job twice. 3.03% of postings, from 1,317 boards read live; 7.60% on boards over 500 postings and zero on boards under 10
