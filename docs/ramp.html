How this was read

One pass over the roster on 2026-09-03: each company's board called once, at its own vendor's public API, unauthenticated. The postings come back carrying the date each one was published, and that date is the whole method — the last 30 days against the 60 before it, normalised to the same length.

A company is called accelerating when it opened at least 3 roles in the last 30 days and did so at twice its prior rate or better. Both conditions matter: the first throws out the noise of very small boards, the second is what separates a ramp from a company that is simply large.

What this cannot see is a posting that was taken down, or a board that went dark — posting dates only record arrivals. That is the one thing here that does need accumulated history, and the daily snapshot series is what collects it.

Scan edges, stated because they are real.
Read in 607s against a 600s wall clock. Greenhouse and Ashby swept complete. Lever reached 493 of 1,538 boards — it publishes Crawl-delay: 1 and we honour it, which is one request per second and 26 minutes for the full list.
So the counts on this page are a floor, not a total. 285,289 postings were read to produce them.
